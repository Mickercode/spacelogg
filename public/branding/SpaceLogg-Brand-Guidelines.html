Logo Usage

✓ Do

Use the full wordmark (SpaceLogg) on light or dark backgrounds with sufficient contrast.

Use the icon mark alone for app icons, favicons, and small placements.

Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "S" on all sides.

✕ Don't

Don't change the colors of the logo or use it on busy backgrounds without a backing.

Don't stretch, rotate, or distort the logo in any direction.

Don't separate "Space" and "Logg" or use them independently in different colors.
