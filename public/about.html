About

Finding your next workspace shouldn't be hard.

SpaceLogg is a workspace discovery platform for remote workers, freelancers, and digital nomads.

Our story

We built SpaceLogg because we know the struggle — you need a quiet spot with fast WiFi, good coffee, and power outlets, but you end up wasting time searching Google, reading outdated reviews, or showing up to a place that doesn't meet your needs.

SpaceLogg brings together coworking spaces, cafes, libraries, and hotel lounges in one place. Browse by city, area, category, or just search for what's nearby. See real reviews from other remote workers, check amenities, view opening hours, and book your spot — all before you leave the house.

What we believe

📍
Local first
We focus on real spaces in real cities. Every listing is verified and community-reviewed.
⚡
No friction
Find, compare, and book a workspace in minutes. No phone calls, no back-and-forth emails.
🤝
Community driven
Reviews and ratings from real users help you pick the right space every time.

For space owners

If you run a coworking space, cafe, library, or hotel lounge — SpaceLogg puts your space in front of thousands of remote workers actively looking for somewhere to work. Contact us to get onboarded and start receiving bookings.
