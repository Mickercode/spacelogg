Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

What we collect

When you use SpaceLogg, we may collect:

  • Account information — name, email address, and password (hashed) when you sign up
  • Booking data — spaces you book, dates, times, and guest counts
  • Reviews — ratings and comments you leave on spaces
  • Location data — only when you use the "Spaces near me" feature, with your permission
  • Payment information — processed securely by Paystack. We do not store card details

How we use it

  • To provide and improve the SpaceLogg service
  • To process bookings and payments
  • To send booking confirmations and account notifications
  • To show you relevant spaces based on your location (with permission)

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with:

  • Space owners — your name and booking details when you make a booking
  • Payment processors — Paystack processes payments on our behalf
  • Email service — Resend delivers transactional emails (confirmations, password resets)

Cookies

We use localStorage to keep you signed in (JWT token). We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

Your rights

You can update your profile information at any time from your account settings. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the email below.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at hello@spacelogg.com
